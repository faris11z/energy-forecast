MLOps Architecture & Verification

1. Ingestion & Leakage Control

Historical hourly load from Bundesnetzagentur / SMARD and 3-city reanalysis weather (Berlin, Frankfurt, Munich). Strict ≥24h lag and rolling window shifting guarantee zero target leakage.

2. Model & Promotion Gate

Regularized LightGBM with 31 leaves. The automated promotion gate only gives a retrained model the production alias if it beats the active model by at least 1% MAE.

3. Drift Monitoring

Seasonally matched KS-test monitoring comparing the last 30 days against the identical window one year prior. Paging activates only when actual forecast accuracy degrades (>5%).
